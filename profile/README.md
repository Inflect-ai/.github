# InflectAI, Inc.

InflectAI, Inc. is a holding company and venture studio that develops and supports focused companies built around Meaning Manifold Dynamics.

The canonical company record is [InflectAI](https://www.inflect-ai.com). GitHub is a public directory for released records, not a mirror of private research or product systems.

## Current public work

- [Vector Manifold Analytics](https://vectormanifold.com) is InflectAI's first company. It applies Meaning Manifold Dynamics to financial markets through the [Financial Market Meaning Manifold](https://vectormanifold.com/fm3), which is in development.
- [Radiant Institute for Manifold Studies](https://www.theradiantinstitute.org) is InflectAI's research institute for public scholarship on meaning, beliefs, threshold dynamics, and systems change. Its [public research index](https://github.com/RadiantManifoldInstitute) is separate from this organization profile.
